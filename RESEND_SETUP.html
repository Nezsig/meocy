Step 1: Add DNS Records in GoDaddy

You need to add 3 records to meocy.com's DNS in GoDaddy: SPF, DKIM, and DMARC.

How to Add DNS Records in GoDaddy:

  1. Go to godaddy.com and sign in
  2. Click Domains (top left menu)
  3. Find meocy.com and click it
  4. Scroll down to DNS section
  5. Click Manage DNS (or Edit)
  6. You'll see a list of existing records. Scroll to the bottom and add new records
---

Step 2: Copy These DNS Records

Add these 3 records exactly as shown:

Record 1: SPF (for sender verification)

Name: @
Type: TXT
Value: v=spf1 include:resend.com ~all

Record 2: DKIM (for email signature)

Name: default._domainkey
Type: CNAME
Value: default.resend.com

Record 3: DMARC (for authentication policy)

Name: _dmarc
Type: TXT
Value: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:postmaster@meocy.com
✅ That's it for DNS! After adding these 3 records, Resend will automatically detect and verify them (can take 5-30 minutes).
---

Step 3: Stop the Catch-All Spam in GoDaddy

The Chinese spam and bounces are caused by a catch-all rule that accepts mail to ANY @meocy.com address. Let's disable it:

How to Remove Catch-All in GoDaddy:

  1. Go to godaddy.com and sign in
  2. Click Domains
  3. Find meocy.com and click it
  4. Click Email (in the left menu, or scroll down)
  5. Look for Forwarding or Email Forwarding
  6. If you see a catch-all rule (usually shows @meocy.com → (some address)), click the trash/delete icon
  7. Click Save
✅ After this: Emails to random @meocy.com addresses will bounce immediately (no spam/backscatter).
